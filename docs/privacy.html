privacy

Nothing about you leaves.

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version.

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no telemetry. Nothing is sent to the person who made it.

What it reads.

The song that is playing, its cover and position, from Windows' own media controls. To move the aurora, it listens to your PC's sound as a few loudness numbers per frame. Nothing is recorded or saved.

What it keeps.

Your settings and a small log in %APPDATA%\Music Notch. Uninstalling deletes both.

Who it talks to.

LRCLIB (lrclib.net), an open lyrics database, gets the song's title, artist, album and length to find lyrics. Turn off Synced lyrics and the app makes no network requests at all.
