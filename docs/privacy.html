Music Notch

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

Music Notch has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing about you is sent to the person who made it.

What it reads on your PC

The title, artist, album, cover art and position of whatever is playing, from Windows' own media controls, the same information the volume flyout shows. To make the aurora move to the music, it listens to your PC's sound output and turns it into a handful of loudness numbers many times a second. That sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your PC

Your settings and a small log file, in %APPDATA%\Music Notch. Uninstalling deletes them.

Who it talks to

LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free and open lyrics database, receives the title, artist, album and length of each song so it can return synced lyrics. Turn off Synced lyrics in the right-click menu and Music Notch makes no network requests at all.
