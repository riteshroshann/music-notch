What it does

Knows the song

Spotify, YouTube in any browser, Apple Music, TIDAL, VLC: anything that shows up in Windows' own media controls shows up here, with its cover art. Nothing playing? The island tucks itself away.

Plays along

Click it and it springs open into a full player: previous, pause, next, and a glowing scrubber you can drag. Space, left and right arrows work too.

Sings and glows

Synced lyrics roll through the notch line by line. The aurora takes its colours from the cover and listens to the actual sound, so the bass hits are light hits.

Set it up in thirty seconds

  1. Install

    Run Music-Notch-win.exe. It installs for you alone, needs no admin rights, and starts straight away. Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Play something

    Hit play in Spotify, or anywhere else. The island drops in from the top of the screen. There's no sign in and nothing to configure.

  3. Make it yours

    Right-click the island, or the tray icon, to turn the aurora, lyrics, sounds or start-at-login on and off.

Uninstall

Open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find Music Notch and choose Uninstall. Uninstall… in the right-click menu takes you straight there. It quits the app and removes it, its start-at-login entry and your settings.

Using the portable zip? Right-click the island, turn off Open at login, quit, then delete the folder.

For IT teams

Install and remove silently, per user:

Music-Notch-win.exe /S
"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\music-notch\Uninstall Music Notch.exe" /S

Where your data goes

The song's title, artist, album and length go to lrclib.net, a free, open lyrics database, to find synced lyrics. Turn lyrics off and even that stops. The sound itself is analysed on your PC for the aurora and never recorded or sent anywhere. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.
